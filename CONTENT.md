## SECTION 1 — FIRST 30 DAYS CHECKLIST

Ordered sequence. Each item: what + key fact + official link.

1. **SG Arrival Card** — all travellers submit within 3 days before arrival (incl. arrival day). Free, via ICA SGAC e-service or MyICA app. Beware paid third-party sites. → https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore/sg-arrival-card
2. **EP/DP formalities** — sequence: employer applies → IPA letter (single-entry visa, valid 6 months) → enter Singapore → medical exam if IPA requires (chest X-ray + HIV, S$30–80 at clinics) → pass issuance → notification letter (1 month validity, can work/travel) → fingerprint/photo at Employment Pass Services Centre (EPSC), by appointment, within 2 weeks of issuance → card delivered within 5 working days. Fees per pass: S$105 application + S$225 issuance. DP processing ~3 weeks; can be filed together with EP. → https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass
3. **Singpass** — register at singpass.gov.sg once passes issued. EP and DP holders (15+) eligible; it's the single digital ID for all government services, tax, clinics, bank verification. Kids too young; wife should register. → https://ask.gov.sg/singpass/questions/clul28lp4002t3b8g3hnggivy
4. **SIM/eSIM** — needs passport or FIN. 2026 SIM-only prices: Circles.Life S$8/mo (500GB), Maxx S$7.90/mo, Simba from S$10/mo, GOMO S$19.99/mo, Singtel/StarHub S$24.50–38/mo. Budget S$10–25/adult/mo. → https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-telco-mobile-plans-singapore-2026
5. **Bank account** — DBS explicitly opens with IPA letter (before EP card); OCBC/UOB vary by branch, often want EP card + Singpass. Docs: passport, EP card or IPA, proof of SG address, Korean TIN. Salary accounts: DBS Multiplier / OCBC 360 / UOB One pay bonus interest for salary crediting + card spend. → https://expatmovingtosingapore.com/expat-friendly-bank-accounts-in-singapore/
6. **PayNow** — link mobile number and/or FIN in the bank app. Free instant transfers; how everyone pays rent deposits, school fees, hawker QR. → https://www.abs.org.sg/consumer-banking/pay-now
7. **Transport / SimplyGo** — contactless Visa/Mastercard works directly on MRT/buses; fares S$1.20–2.80/ride. **Kids: under 7 and ≤0.9m ride free; under 7 but taller than 0.9m ride free with a free Child Concession Card** (any SimplyGo Ticket Office, passport accepted, foreign kids eligible). Both twins ride free — get 2 cards once they pass 0.9m. → https://simplygo.com.sg/travel-fares/child-concessionary-fares/
8. **Preschool visits & waitlists** — start immediately; popular centres have 12–18 month waitlists (see Section 2).
9. **Korean driving licence conversion** — drive on Korean licence + IDP/official translation for first 12 months of residence; convert before that ends. Pass Basic Theory Test (50 MCQs, 45/50 to pass, S$10.14/attempt at BBDC/CDC/SSDC) → conversion at Traffic Police HQ, 10 Ubi Ave 3, S$50 fee. Korean licence needs official English translation from a Singapore-registered translation company (or embassy certification). Total ~S$70–80, card ~2 weeks. → https://www.btt.sg/en/blog/how-to-convert-foreign-driving-licence-singapore
10. **재외국민등록 (Overseas Korean Registration)** — Korean citizens abroad 90+ days must register with the embassy; online via 영사민원24/G4K or at the consulate. Embassy of the Republic of Korea: 47 Scotts Road, Goldbell Towers, S228233 (⚠️VERIFY unit number). → https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/sg-ko/wpge/m_2505/contents.do

### DP / visa facts (slim section)
- EP holder needs **min. S$6,000/mo fixed salary** to sponsor spouse + children under 21 on DPs. → https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/eligibility
- Documents: marriage certificate (spouse), birth certificates showing parents' names (kids). Korean documents need **original + English translation uploaded as one file** (no apostille requirement stated by MOM). Tip: 가족관계증명서/혼인관계증명서/기본증명서 can be issued in English via 정부24. → https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/documents-required
- **DP spouse work rule (2026): the general Letter of Consent is gone** — a DP spouse who wants to work must qualify for her own EP/S Pass/Work Permit on her own merits. Only exception: LOC for DP business owners (sole proprietor/partner/director with ≥30% shareholding). → https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/loc-for-dependants-pass-business-owners/eligibility
- **Child immunisation gate**: diphtheria + measles proof is mandatory for foreign-born children ≤12 applying for DP — verified via CDA/NIR before MOM application (~10 working days). Korean records accepted if they show vaccine types + dates; non-English records need official translation; Korean 예방접종증명서 can be issued in English via 정부24/보건소. After arrival, update records via NIR e-service. → https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/documentation-of-diphtheria-and-measles-vaccination-required-for-foreign-born-children-applying-for-long-term-immigration-passes-in-singapore/ and https://www.nir.cda.gov.sg/eservices/submitNewVaccMyChildWithoutLoginPage
- EP context (two-liner): 2026 minimum qualifying salary S$5,600/mo (rises with age to S$10,700 at 45+; financial services higher); **rises 1 Jan 2027 to S$6,000–11,500** — relevant at renewal. COMPASS points framework also applies. No CPF for foreigners (salary is gross); resident income tax progressive 0–24%. → https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility and https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-residency-and-tax-rates/individual-income-tax-rates

### 2b. Primary school paths (P1 = year child turns 7; twins born ~2022–2024 → P1 ~2029–2031)

**No official school tiers exist in Singapore** — frame all tier language as informal expat/parent convention.

**Path 1 — Local (MOE) schools.** Honest framing: international students register in Phase 3, after ALL citizens/PRs. Must submit an online indication of interest (2026 window was 19–25 May; missing it = automatic rejection). If offered, MOE assigns a designated school — **no school choice, no appeal**; vacancies are essentially in less-subscribed neighbourhood schools (~69 schools had Phase 3 openings in 2024). Fees for international students (non-ASEAN): **S$1,035/mo in 2026** (rising annually) — still far cheaper than international schools. ⚠️Twins are separate applicants; no same-school guarantee in Phase 3 — verify with MOE. The informal "popular tier" (Nan Hua, Rosyth, Pei Hwa Presbyterian, Nanyang etc., which ballot even for citizens) is effectively unavailable to internationals; the tier talk matters mainly if the family later gets PR. → https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/international-students and https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/press-releases/20231018-revised-school-fees-for-non-citizens-in-government-and-government-aided-schools-for-2024-to-2026 and https://livefree.sg/most-popular-primary-schools-in-singapore/

### 3.1 Housing types — a newcomer's decoder (render as its own explainer box/cards)

- **HDB flat** — public housing towers where ~80% of Singaporeans live. Whole-flat rental to foreigners is legal (quota per block). Sizes: 3-room ≈ 700 sqft, 4-room ≈ 970 sqft, 5-room ≈ 1,200 sqft. No pool/gym/security, but hawker centres, shops and playgrounds at the doorstep, and 30–50% cheaper than condos. Minimum 6-month tenancy. → https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/renting-a-flat/renting-from-the-open-market/eligibility
- **Condominium** — private development with security, pool, gym, playground, BBQ pits; management fees are the landlord's problem. What most expat families rent. Leasehold (99-yr) vs freehold matters to buyers, not tenants — what matters to a tenant is the **build era**: pre-2012 condos have bigger units and helper's rooms (see 3.5).
- **Executive Condominium (EC)** — HDB-condo hybrid in the suburbs that becomes fully private after 10 years; rents and looks like a condo. Just know the label.
- **Walk-up apartment** — older low-rise (3–4 storeys), no lift, no facilities; big square footage for the money. Common in Katong/Joo Chiat.
- **Landed** — houses: **terrace** (row house, shared side walls) → **semi-detached** (one shared wall) → **detached/bungalow** → **Good Class Bungalow (GCB)** (plots ≥1,400 sqm; Singapore's most exclusive housing, S$16k–100k+/mo to rent). Tenant usually takes on garden/pool upkeep; older houses may lack central aircon. No shared facilities, no security.
- **Cluster house** — strata landed: a landed-style house (often 3–4 storeys) inside a gated development that shares condo-style facilities (pool, security). Best-of-both for families wanting space + a pool; supply is thin and concentrated in pockets (Toh Tuck/Eng Kong near Beauty World, Chestnut/Hillview — e.g., Chestnut Residences). → https://www.singaporeexpats.com/condo/cluster-house/2555/CHESTNut-RESIDENCES (⚠️fix URL case: /CHESTNUT-RESIDENCES)
- **Conservation shophouse** — heritage two/three-storey with shops below, homes above; character living in Katong/Joo Chiat and Emerald Hill.
- **Serviced apartment** — furnished, min. 7-day stays; the standard landing pad for the first 1–2 months while hunting.

### 3.2 Market context (2026)
Overall roughly flat: 2025 index +1.9%; 2026 quarterly moves small and mixed (Q2 2026: CCR +1.2%, RCR 0.0%, OCR −0.3%; vacancy 6.4%; ~61k units in pipeline). Practical line: **tenant-friendly in supply-heavy suburbs, firm in prime central; landlords of a ~S$6k 3BR typically accepting ~S$500 below asking in 2026; transacted rents ~3–8% below asking**. → https://propertynet.sg/private-rents-rise-suburban-condo-prices-dip-q2-2026-landlords-tenants/ and https://www.propertyguru.com.sg/property-guides/singapore-condo-rental-prices-q1-2026-pjx-98395

### 3.6 The helper's-room rule of thumb (callout box)
New-launch 3BRs (post-~2012) are compact (900–1,100 sqft) and usually have **no helper's room** — a live-in helper means hunting for "3BR + utility/yard" in **pre-2012 condos** (Maplewoods, Signature Park, Sommerville Park, The Esta, One Amber, Park Infinia, Chancery Court era) or paying for a 4BR in new stock. Search terms that matter on PropertyGuru/99.co: "utility room", "yard", "+study". → https://stackedhomes.com/editorial/shrinking-3-bedroom-new-condo-sizes-how-much-smaller-can-it-go/

### 3.7 Which sub-area? (decision block — present as guidance, not a verdict)
- **Priority = Korean community + school**: Beauty World/Upper BT — SKIS walkable, Sol Mart at Bukit Timah Plaza; accept older-mall retail and a ~40-min office commute. (The Reserve Residences mall/interchange upgrades this story from 2028.)
- **Priority = office commute + lifestyle**: East Coast (Marine Parade/Amber) — TEL straight into the office basement, beach weekends; accept the cross-island school run, so plan local preschool.
- **Priority = balance on one line**: KAP/Sixth Avenue — 5 min to SKIS, ~30–40 min DTL to the office, elite-school belt for the local-primary long shot; pay D10 prices for thin condo supply.
- **Priority = space per dollar in the school orbit**: Hillview — S$1,500–2,000/mo cheaper, HillV2/CS Fresh lifestyle; accept the longer DTL ride and check the MRT walk per condo.
- **Priority = expat network for the wife**: Holland Village — deepest expat-family bench, now one-seat to Marina Bay; highest rents of the realistic options.
- **Priority = urban convenience**: Newton/Novena — shortest MRT ride, enrichment mall, big older units with helper rooms; least neighbourhood feel, and the car route (CTE) carries Singapore's priciest ERP.

### 3.8 How renting works here (box)
- Lease: 12 or 24 months standard (24 common for families); almost no landlord takes <12. Legal minimum stay 3 months (private) / 6 months (HDB).
- Deposit: 1 month per year of lease (2 months on a 2-year lease); returned 7–30 days after end, less damage beyond fair wear.
- Agent commission: if rent > ~S$3,500/mo the landlord pays; below that, tenant pays ~half month per year. Pay agency by cheque/transfer, never cash to the agent.
- **Diplomatic clause: essential for EP holders** — break after 12 months with 2 months' notice if job ends/relocates (paired with pro-rated commission refund clause).
- Minor repairs: tenant covers first ~S$150–200 per item (negotiate the cap). Aircon: tenant takes quarterly servicing contract, keep receipts.
- Process: view → Letter of Intent + good-faith deposit (1 month, converts to deposit) → sign Tenancy Agreement. Good units go in days. Docs: passport + EP or IPA letter. Stamp duty (tenant): 0.4% of total rent.
- Utilities: SP Group account; family of four ~S$250–400/mo incl. aircon-heavy use + broadband S$30–50 (500Mbps–1Gbps).
→ https://www.singaporeexpats.com/guides-for-expats/procedure-for-rental.htm and https://www.propertyguru.com.sg/property-guides/tenancy-agreement-security-deposit-utility-deposit-in-singapore-9515 and https://www.smartcalculator.sg/articles/singapore-utilities-cost-2026

## SECTION 6 — HIRING A HELPER

- EP families are eligible; first-time employers take MOM's Employers' Orientation Programme (≥2 working days before WP application).
- **Levy: S$300/mo standard rate.** The S$60 concessionary rate does NOT apply — it requires a Singapore-citizen child; agency sites often gloss over this. → https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/levy-concession
- Salary (2026 monthly): Filipino S$620–850; Indonesian S$550–780; Myanmar S$550–750; experienced transfer helpers S$750–1,000+. → https://upwill.com.sg/resources/maid-salary
- Upfront: agency fee S$1,000–3,000 + WP fees S$70 + medical S$40–150 + 26-month insurance S$400–800 ≈ **S$3,000–5,000 one-time**. Compulsory: medical insurance ≥S$60k/yr, personal accident ≥S$60k, S$5,000 security bond (discharged via insurer). Settling-In Programme S$76–93 for first-time MDWs. → https://www.singsaver.com.sg/blog/cost-guide-to-hiring-a-domestic-helper-maid-singapore
- Obligations: food, adequate private lodging, medical care; budget S$200–350/mo living costs.
- **All-in monthly ≈ S$1,150–1,550.**
- Timeline: transfer helper (already in SG) ~2 weeks; fresh overseas hire 6–8+ weeks — **with twins, start the search before or immediately on arrival.** → https://www.jazhelpers.com.sg/guides/how-long-hiring-a-helper-takes

## SECTION 7 — THE CAR QUESTION

- **COE (Aug 2026, 1st bidding): Cat A S$123,890; Cat B S$129,910** — the certificate alone, before the car. Live tracker: https://www.motorist.sg/coe-results
- All-in: Corolla-class family car ≈ **S$170k–220k on the road** (COE ≈ 64% of price); true monthly cost incl. depreciation ≈ **S$2,300–3,000** (running costs alone S$700–1,200: road tax, insurance S$120–200, petrol ~S$3.16/L, parking, ERP, servicing). → https://www.smartcalculator.sg/articles/how-much-car-cost-singapore-2026
- **Leasing** (all-in, no COE risk): compact S$1,400–1,800/mo; family SUV S$2,000–2,800/mo. → https://www.freshcars.sg/blog/car-leasing-cost-singapore-price-breakdown-2026
- **Vs Grab/taxi**: cross-town S$8–18; heavy family use ≈ S$400–800/mo — far below car cost. Verdict for an MRT-adjacent family: skip the car.
- **Child seat rules (twins under 1.35m)**: child restraint mandatory in private cars AND Grab/private-hire (NOT exempt — S$150 fine + 3 demerit points). **Taxis ARE exempt** (rear seat). Options: GrabFamily (car-seat-equipped, ~S$2–5 extra, limited availability), carry portable seats, or use taxis (CDG Zig app). → https://singaporelegaladvice.com/car-seat-rules-singapore/
